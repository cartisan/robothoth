## Motivation
- strong feedback culture have higher employee retention rates and engagement scores
- Johari's windows:

|  | known to self | not known to self |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
|known to others      | Open area      |  blind spot     |
|not known to others        | Facade      | unknown     |

- covering your blind spot can only happen with help from outside
- giving/receiving feedback is hard --> we only learn this by doing

## Giving Feedback
- Ask yourself first: What do you want to achieve by giving this feedback?
	- Recognition: Kudos for sth well done
	- Evaluation: judging work performance and results
	- Coaching: helping someone develop
- Approach: HIP-AID
	- **H**: Humble and Helpful - should come from a well intended place, assume good intentions
	- **I**: Immediate - should be given on the spot and not days after
	- **P**: 
		- Public / Private - Praise should be public, criticism private 
		- Not Personality - Feedback should be about observable behavior, not about personality which is intransient 
	- **A**: Action - what is an obseveable behavior, give examples and context
	- **I**: Impact - what is the impact of the behavior on me/the project/...
	- **D**: Dos - what should the other person do to help me with this impact

## Receiving Feedback
- Framing of feedback:
	- Fixed mindset: Feedback is *criticism* of me as a person
	- Growth mindset: Feedback *helps me* grow as a person
- Remember: they care about our relationship if the provide feedback
- Listen actively and be curious, with a focus on understanding
- Explore how this can help you
- Take time to process thee feedback, request time to follow up
- Agree to disagree, and say when you would not like to take the feedback
- Ask for help
