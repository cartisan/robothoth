## Observation
- Knowledge work seems ephemeral, results often disappear or stay unconnected
	- A marking in a book with some margin notes
	- A power point with ideas presented to a group of peers
	- Transient notes, that are not connected or processed
- Mental load is required to keep ideas present and connected, which does not scale

![[dcf07e6a-7b8f-4329-a382-ceed151c4d15_750x751.jpg]]

## Insight
- We should implement knowledge work practices which accumulate knowledge over time
	- Ideas should be reified and allow operationalization as well as organization
	- Fleeting notes are for structuring thought, not for knowledge working
	- [[Note taking is thinking]]
		- individual notes are ideas
		- note taking is about managing note network
