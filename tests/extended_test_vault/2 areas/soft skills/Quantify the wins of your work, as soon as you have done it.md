## Record the impact while context is available
Include meaningful numbers in a [[Performance Self Review]]. It is difficult to
reconstruct the impact later when data and context have changed. Evaluate the
outcome of a substantial work package while preparing its [[Work Log]] entry.

## Generic examples
- Measure a routine job's runtime before and after a maintenance change.
- Compare counts of repeated manual steps before and after automation.
- Record whether a refactor made a common development task easier, and explain
  the measurement's limitations rather than inventing a financial impact.

Keep the outcome distinct from the amount of implementation work performed.
