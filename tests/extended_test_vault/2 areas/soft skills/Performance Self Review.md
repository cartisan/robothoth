- To come prepared to a performance interview one should do a self review
	- have an opinion yourself about where you stand
	- have material to argue your case
- Self reviews can help manager do their review, as it lists one's accomplishments which the manager has to assemble anyways when [[Giving Performance Reviews]]
	- Enables fairer reviews
## Structure
1. Goals and Expectations for period
	- Ideally they were agreed with the manager previously
	- Should relate to companies progression framework
2. List of accomplishments ("What I did")
	- Order by priority, and relate to goals/expectations
	- Use numbers where meaningful
		- number of services changed or projects owned
		- number of people collaborated with or mentored
		- number of interviews made or homeworks graded
		- business impact: revenue, reliability, efficiency
	- Link to specifics
		- MRs or code reviews
		- RFC's started or design docs created
	- Add link to current [[Work Log]]
3. List of soft contributions ("How I contributed and collaborated")
	- qualitative details of work - things without big business impact but improving team or company
	- probably your manager does not know
	- Examples:
		- list people helped within and outside the team
		- mention knowledge management and sharing efforts
		- process improvements
4. Reflect on levels and competencies
	- where does one stand and why
	- if high trust, might share self-ratings with manager
