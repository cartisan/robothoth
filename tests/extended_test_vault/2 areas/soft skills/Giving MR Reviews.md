## Semantic comments
The idea of semantic comments is that the reviewer prepends every comment with a label to clearly express their intent and expectations.
### Labels
- **Remark**: I have a simple remark, no change or answer is expected from my side.
- **Question**: I have an honest question and I expect an answer.
- **Hint**: I don't expect any change or answer in this review, but you may find this interesting and consider it for the future.
- **Suggestion**: I have a suggestion and I expect that you think about it. Whether you accept it or not is up to you.
- **Important**: This is important to me and I expect a change. If you strongly disagree I expect that we discuss this further.
- **Crucial**: This code must not be merged. It has a severe issue and must be changed in order to avert harm.
