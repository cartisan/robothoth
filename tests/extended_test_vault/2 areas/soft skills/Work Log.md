- Have a note that  lists big and small achievements for a period of time
	- One document with weekly entries, perhaps also one which aggregates that into quarterly buckets
	- Don't forget fuzzy "soft" contributions
		- Things like: improve code quality, make on call easier, ...
		- Very likely to fall under the radar
		- List small contributions under headings of what they achieved

## Rationale
- Easy to forget minor wins and soft contributions
- Helps compile a [[Performance Self Review]]
- Helps feeling productive and reflecting on the themes of one's work
	- Is there a narrative to one's work, how does it fit together?
	--> Have sections under which your achievements are thematically grouped
	- Questions that can be asked:
		- **What work do I feel most proud of?**
		- **Are there themes in these projects I should be thinking about?**
		- **What do I wish I was doing more / less of?**
		- **Which of my projects had the effect I wanted, and which didn’t?**
