**Goal**: To encourage behavior in others, change their internal state

- Easiest way to change state, is to tell a god story
	- Story: Come up with an idea, show that idea is worth pursuing and possible
- Companies are stories, too
	- Startup doesn't have full product, so we need to build a story (=vision) and choose to fully believe/embody it
	- Sales: ignite customers to believe the same story
	- When you tell a story, be aware which archetype you are while telling, also which archetype your company is
		- Wise Old Friend, Hero, Jester, Explorer, ...
- How to communicate:
	- *Why* -> *How* -> *What* (The Golden Circle by Simon Sinek)
	- From *Functions* to *Benefits* to *Values* (think Maslov)
		- e.g. Nokia vs Apple
			- 32 GB -> Safe more stuff -> Safety (never run out of space)
