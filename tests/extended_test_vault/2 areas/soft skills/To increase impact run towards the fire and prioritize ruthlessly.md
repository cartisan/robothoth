## Definition
On a high level, there are two major ways we can have impact:
1. **Reducing Cost** - Making things cheaper; doing more with less.
2. **Increasing Revenue** - Improving product quality, contributing to user growth, or creating new revenue streams.

## Maximizing impact
- Run towards the fire
	- Natural tendencies is to avoid problems, but problems are opportunities 
	- Identify problems and learn to judge severity
	- Take responsibility of problems with high severity and fix them -> develops a reputation
- prioritize ruthlessly
	- take on work with highest impact and be clear what you will not do
	- priority = benefit / cost or effort
	- best task are: impactfull and low cost 
- Be scrappy
	- own your projects and do what you can to make progress
	- if delegation costs to much time, don't be afraid to jump into unfamiliar tasks and code bases
	- only applies to simple work outside your domain
