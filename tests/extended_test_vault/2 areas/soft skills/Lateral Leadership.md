## Tenets
- **Never surprise your manager**
	- Surprising your manager (in big ways) destroys trust
		- surprises disrupt you manager's rhythm while they juggle many tasks
	- Proactively communicate blockers or potential problems to prepare your manager
		- make clear you are "feeding context", not bringing problems to solve
	- Treat every surprise as an incident and learn from it
- **Don't let your manager surprise you**
	- Managers juggle many tasks and can forget to relay important information
	- Proactively maintain information flow on tasks relevant to you
		- use 1:1s and topical meetings to prod for information
		- follow up when you know your manager was bound to receive relevant information
	- Regularly check whether your and your manager's priorities align
- **Feed your manager context**
	- Help your manager not get surprised by the wider organization
		- make clear you are "feeding context", not bringing problems to solve
