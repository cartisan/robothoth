		- E.g. in first screening call: history of Example Company
		- in team interview: regular team weeks, home office
		- in tech interview: tech stuff
- Question techniques:
	- generally use open questions
		- w-questions: when, why, how, with what
		- not answerable with yes/no
	- close questions
		- only answerable with yes/no
		- can be used when you want to go deeper, and get an important answer
		- don't mix with open questions
	- types of questions:
		- focus on *past behavior*: "past behavior is best predictor of future behavior"
			- "Describe a situation when..."
		- if candidate has not much experience, ask about *future/potential*
			- "Imagine a situation when"
		- *technical*: 
			- "do you use X and why?"
			- "describe how you would..."
