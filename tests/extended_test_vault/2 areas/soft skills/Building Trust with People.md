Why trust matters:
- 78% b2b buyers wont buy without trust
- 20% cost reduction per trust point
- 3.5x faster deal closing

Trust equation: ![[Pasted image 20260409113704.png]]
- *Credibility*: Presenting facts, and as they are (Does my client see me as expert?)
	- Factors
		- credentials
		- honesty
	- Say no, if you don't know something
	- Ask informed questions
	- Be specific
	- Admit mistakes early and loudly 
- *Reliability*: Actions happen as promised
	- Under-promise and over-deliver, but use strategically so people won't get used to it
	- Manage expectations: if commitment can't be kept, notify early
	- Confirmation loops: quick follow up after verbal agreements
	- Consistency also in small things: being on time, updating about project proactively
	- Avoid hero complex: failed "yes" hurts more than respectful "no"
	- Consistent performance
- *Intimacy*: Is Information they share safe (wont be spread, used against them)
	- Being approachable and discreet, make people feel heard and not managed
	- Create a safe space for tough conversations
	- Active empathy: try understanding why people feel what they feel
	- Micro Vulnerability: show you have feelings
	- Don't spread sensitive information
	- Don't misuse or attack a person when they have been vulnerable, using what they shared
- *Self-Orientation*: Am I perceived as acting mostly on own interest, or shared interest
	- Shift from I to We: focus on shared goals instead of only what I need
	- Be transparent about own incentives: don't hide motives, or people will get suspicious
		- Paradoxically, sharing incentives openly creates trust
	- Focus on Service: "How can I be of most service to the people in this room, right now?"
