## Summary
- Computation: $$1-\frac{error(model)}{variance(data)}$$
- Bound between -inf and 1, with 0 being the score of a baseline always predicting the expected value
- The proportion of the variance in the dependent variable that is predictable from the independent variable.
- Key nuance:
	- Unlike [[Correlation - Pearson's r|r]], $R^2$ *does not indicate direction* , it focuses strictly on goodness-of-fit.
