## Goals
- understand how to prevent similar incidents in the future
- share knowledge about how to investigate incidents

## Best Practices
- Keep it blameless
- At beginning:
	- a unified platform to share knowledge: insights, graphs, query results (and code)
	- a thinking tool to help further the investigation
- Properties of finished reports:
	- should be public and easy to find
	- should be easy to read and understand
	- should uncover which tools and steps were used to investigate the incident
