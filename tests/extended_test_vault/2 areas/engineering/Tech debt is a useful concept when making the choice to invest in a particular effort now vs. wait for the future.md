## Debt metaphor
- We can invest in something today, or we can borrow against the future and plan to invest later
- The costs will be higher to address a problem later, but we gain more flexibility in the short term

## Negotiating time for tech dent
- Concept not helpful when negotiating time for doing something
	- The concept is to broad: business might be reluctant to schedule time for it again and again not seeing the different nature of the returns on investment
	- Use more precise label, because that makes clear the business value of scheduling time and shows that work is done on different things
- Concept is helpful when making the decision to *take on* tech debt
	-  timing of an investment and its cost are connected
		- We gain resources required for opportunity costs of tech debt solution
		- We will have to pay higher costs later, and need to be able to pay them
