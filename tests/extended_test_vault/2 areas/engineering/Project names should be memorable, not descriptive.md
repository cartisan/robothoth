Projects should have cute/memorable names, instead of descriptive names:
"The problem comes in when there's a mismatch between responsibilities and names. Names are a way of _expressing identity_, while responsibilities are ephemeral: Your friend Sam is still Sam, even if Sam gets new responsibilities and sheds old ones."
(https://ntietz.com/blog/name-your-projects-cutesy-things/)
