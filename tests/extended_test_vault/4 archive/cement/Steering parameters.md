* `Q` is pass-through (Durchgang) and `R = 1-Q` is remainder (Rückstand) in %
* `D` is diameter of sieve in µm
* `d'` is diameter of sieve at which 63.2% Durchgang is achieved

* all particle size distributions follow RRSB distribution
	* Two parameters: slope `n` and inflection point `d'` 
	* At `d'` the RRSB distribution is statistically the widest
