## Process introduction
- Problems:
	- shortcut epics/objectives not well defined
	- disconnect of product roadmap and monthly planning
	- we mix planning implementation and strategic goal setting
		- planning how we do a thing while figuring out what we do
	- refinement/shaping underused
- Goals:
	- clear planning process based on team goals
	- not working on under-shaped work packages
	- a shared view for planning ahead

## Shape Up Deep Dive
- three phases
	- framing:
		- identify and validate the business relevance of problems
		- have idea of complexity and urgency
	- shaping:
		- develop detailed and actionable plan, while allowing for creative solutions
		- turn raw idea -> shaped pitch
			- scope is defined but not implementation details
	- building:
		- turn shaped plan into shippable feature
- ceremonies
	- quarterly goal review
	- 3 week iterations and 1 week down time
	- iteration:
		- only work on stories and epics we shaped and planned for during iteration
		- try to get the planned work done, do not carry things to next iteration
		- break down shaped work further
	- down time:
		- demo, retro, betting and planning meetings
		- work on tech debt and out of order tasks
		- time for personal development
	- ideation, framing and shaping can happen any time
- concepts:
	- appetite: instead of estimating cost determine how much you are willing to spend
	- work intentionally instead of reactively
	- engineers adjust solution path dynamically
	- creative funnel: not everything that is part of funnel at beginning  also makes it to the end, there is drop off
