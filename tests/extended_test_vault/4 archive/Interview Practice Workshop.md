## Practice interview behavior
In a role-play, give the interviewer enough to explore without making every
answer artificially perfect. Try incomplete context, overly broad answers, or
answers that require a follow-up about personal responsibility and outcomes.

## Observations
- <interviewer-a> introduced the conversation's context clearly.
- <interviewer-b> used a leading question; an open question would leave more
  room for the participant's own account.
- <interviewer-c> asked for a concrete example when <participant-a> stayed general.
- Ask what the participant personally contributed, rather than treating a group
  outcome as evidence of individual responsibility.
- Avoid allowing a salary role-play to consume the entire discussion.

## Practice questions
- What was the context, and what did you personally do?
- What obstacle mattered most, and how did you respond?
- What changed as a result, and what would you do differently?
